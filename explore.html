Rose

Rose flower

Roses in India hold a special place, symbolising love, beauty, and purity. From religious ceremonies to weddings and festive occasions, various types of rose plants in India play vital roles in traditions. It is also said that Goddess Lakshmi’s blessings grace homes adorned with roses, adding prosperity and joy.

Lily flower

lily, (genus Lilium), genus of 80 to 100 species of herbaceous flowering plants of the family Liliaceae, native to temperate areas of the Northern Hemisphere. Many lilies are prized as ornamental plants, and they have been extensively hybridized.
