School Subject Descriptions

Innovation Lab:
A subject where you design cool projects — coding apps, building models, or experimenting with robotics. It’s all about turning ideas into reality.
Creative Arts & Expression:
Beyond drawing and painting, this includes music, dance, drama, and digital art. A space to express yourself freely and discover hidden talents.
Adventure Science:
Science taught through experiments, outdoor activities, and fun challenges. Instead of just reading, you explore how the world works hands‑on.
Life Skills & Fun Learning:
A subject that teaches teamwork, problem‑solving, finance basics, cooking, or even chess. It prepares you for real life while keeping things playful.
