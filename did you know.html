Fun facts about ocean

The Ocean Covers Most of Earth

Oceans cover about 71% of the Earth's surface and contain roughly 96.5% of all Earth's water, making them the largest habitat on the planet and a critical component of the global water cycle.

Half of Earth's Oxygen Comes from the Ocean

Tiny organisms like plankton and algae produce around 50% of the oxygen we breathe, highlighting the ocean’s essential role in sustaining life on Earth.

The Deepest Parts Are Astonishingly Deep

The Mariana Trench in the Pacific Ocean is the deepest point on Earth at nearly 7 miles (6.8 miles) deep, which is deeper than the height of Mount Everest
