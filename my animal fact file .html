Bengal Tiger

Type:Big Cat

Lives in:Bengal tigers live across India, Bangladesh, Nepal, and Bhutan, inhabiting tropical and subtropical forests, tall grasslands, and the mangrove swamps of the Sundarbans.

Diet:Deer and wild pigs

Cool Fact: Bengal tigers are the largest wild cats in the world, known for their striking stripes, powerful hunting skills, and remarkable adaptability.!
