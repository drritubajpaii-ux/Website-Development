Warm-Up Exercise

Start with light body movements and stretches to prepare muscles before exercising.

Walk at a fast pace to improve heart health, increase energy, and burn calories.

Time: 30 minutes

Body Strength Drill

Perform squats, lunges, and simple push-ups to strengthen muscles and improve fitness.

Time: 20 minutes

Meditation & Yoga

Practise breathing exercises and yoga poses to relax the mind and improve concentration.

Time: 15 minutes

Cycling Session

Enjoy cycling outdoors or indoors to build endurance and keep the body active.

Time: 25 minutes

Recovery Time

Take proper rest, stretch lightly, and drink water to help your body recover.

Time: Throughout the day
