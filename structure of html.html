The Wispering Lantern

In a quiet village nestled between misty hills, there stood an old lantern hanging from a crooked tree. Every night, it glowed softly, though no one ever lit it.

One evening, a curious girl named Amara touched the lantern, and it whispered her name. Startled, she listened closely as it spoke of hidden paths and forgotten dreams. Guided by its light, Amara followed the winding trail into the forest, where shadows danced like secrets waiting to be told.

At the heart of the woods, she found a crystal-clear pond. The lantern’s glow reflected on the water, revealing not monsters or treasure, but her own courage shimmering back at her. From that night on, Amara knew the lantern’s magic was not in its flame, but in awakening the bravery she carried all along
