Building a Healthy Plate

Why a Balanced Meal Matters

A balanced meal matters because it fuels your body with the right mix of nutrients, keeps your energy steady, and protects you from long‑term health problems. Think of it like giving your body the perfect toolkit—it has everything it needs to grow, repair, and stay strong.

Benefits of a Balanced Meal

  • Provides steady energy throughout the day
  • Supports growth and immunity
  • Prevents lifestyle diseases
  • Improves mood and concentration

Example of a Balanced Plate

Half plate: Fruits & Vegetables
Quarter plate: Whole Grains
Quarter plate: Proteins
Small portion: Healthy Fats

Smart Snack Choices

Snacks are not just fillers between meals—they can be a smart way to fuel your body and mind. Choosing the right snacks helps maintain energy, focus, and overall health.

✅ Benefits of Smart Snacks

  • Boost energy during study or play
  • Prevent overeating at mealtimes
  • Provide essential nutrients
  • Support healthy growth and concentration

🥗 Examples of Smart Snacks

  • Fresh fruits like apples, bananas, or grapes
  • Vegetable sticks with hummus
  • Whole‑grain crackers with cheese
  • Nuts and seeds in small portions
  • Yogurt with berries

Nutrition Tips for Students

Good nutrition helps students stay energetic, focused, and healthy. Making smart food choices can improve memory, concentration, and overall performance in school.

✅ Key Nutrition Tips

  • Eat a balanced breakfast to start the day strong
  • Include fruits and vegetables in every meal
  • Choose whole grains like oats, brown rice, or whole‑wheat bread
  • Drink plenty of water instead of sugary drinks
  • Snack smart with nuts, yogurt, or fruit
  • Avoid junk food and fried snacks

Different colours in fruits and vegetables give different nutrients. A colourful plate often means a healthier plate.
